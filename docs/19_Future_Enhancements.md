# 19. Future Enhancements

1. **Student Login**: Provide a portal for students to view their own normalized marks and analytics.
2. **Automated Excel Export**: Allow admin and faculty to export the analytics tables and final marks back into standard Excel formats for university printing.
3. **Email Notifications**: Automatically email faculty when their account is approved, or email students when marks are finalized.
4. **Machine Learning**: Predict student failure risk based on Internal 1 and 2 trends.
