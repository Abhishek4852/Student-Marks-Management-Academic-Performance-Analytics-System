# 3. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder Role | Responsibilities | Interests & Expectations | System Interactions | Influence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Administrator** | Manage programs, batches, students, semesters, subjects, and faculty approvals. | Requires a bird's-eye view of all academic operations and bulk-import capabilities. | Manages all foundational data; views global analytics. | High |
| **Faculty Member** | Enter and update marks for assigned subjects. | Requires secure access, easy marks entry interfaces, and subject-level performance analytics. | Enters marks; views class analytics. | Medium |
| **Academic Dept.** | Oversee academic policies. | Expects accurate, tamper-proof academic records and analytics. | Receives exported reports/analytics. | High |
| **Students** | Beneficiaries of the system. | Expect their marks to be calculated and recorded accurately and fairly. | None directly (managed by Admin/Faculty). | Low (Passive) |
